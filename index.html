About the program

Support that starts with you

Akan’s Youth Transitions to Adulthood (YTA) Program supports young people aged 15 to 22 as they move into independent adulthood. It’s for youth who are leaving foster care or group care, or who are heading into adulthood without steady support from family.

You work one-on-one with a youth worker. Together you set your own goals and take the next step at your pace.

What we offer

Support for the things that matter on your own

Eight areas of support, shaped around your goals.

  • Independent living and life skills

    Practical skills for running a home and a daily routine.

  • Housing and accommodation

    Help to find and keep a safe place to live.

  • Financial literacy and budgeting

    Understand money and plan with confidence.

  • Employment readiness and career development

    Get ready for work and grow a career.

  • Education planning

    Plan your next step in school or training.

  • Access to community resources

    Find services and supports near you.

  • Healthy relationships and support networks

    Grow your circle of support.

  • Mental health and addiction connections

    Be connected to care when you need it.
