About supporting the program

At Akan’s Youth Transitions to Adulthood (YTA) Program, we believe that the right support, guidance and opportunities can make a lasting difference in a young person’s life.

Your interest in supporting our work helps strengthen a community where young people are encouraged to discover their potential, develop essential life skills and take meaningful steps toward independence.

On this page: Why your support matters · Be part of their journey · Making a contribution · Questions

Why Your Support Matters

Transitioning into adulthood can bring challenges, particularly for young people without consistent family support.

Through individualized guidance and practical support, Akan’s YTA helps young people work toward:

  • Greater confidence and independence.
  • Essential budgeting and financial management skills.
  • Housing stability and independent living.
  • Education and employment opportunities.
  • Healthy relationships and supportive networks.
  • Connections to important community resources.
