The YTA Program

A steady partner as you step into adulthood

One-on-one support from a youth worker who works with your goals.

Who it’s for

The YTA Program is for young people aged 15 to 22 who are:

  • leaving foster care or group care, or
  • heading into adulthood without steady support from family.

Not sure if you fit? Reach out. We’d rather talk it through with you.

What you can expect

  • A youth worker who gets to know you and listens first.
  • One-on-one support built around your own goals.
  • Help with everyday things like housing, money, school and work.
  • Connections to people and services in your community.

Eligibility

Participants are aged 15 to 22. Any further criteria are still being confirmed.
